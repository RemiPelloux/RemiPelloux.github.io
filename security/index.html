CVE-2026-45753, Symfony HtmlSanitizer

Symfony's HtmlSanitizer strips dangerous markup from untrusted HTML. One class, UrlAttributeSanitizer, checks the scheme of every URL attribute. Its list of attributes held src, href, lowsrc, background and ping. It left out action, formaction, poster and cite, so a javascript: URI in one of those four went through untouched and could run as XSS.

The default allowSafeElements() configuration is not affected. The bug needs a configuration that allows one of those attributes on purpose, such as a form with its action.

Affected
symfony/html-sanitizer from 6.1.0, before 6.4.40, 7.4.12 and 8.0.12

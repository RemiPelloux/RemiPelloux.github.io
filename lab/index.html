Lab

Six small things that run in the page. Each one comes from a project of mine or from this site.

  1. 01A seeded runThe idea behind Modelly, in 100 lines.
  2. 02Commit rhythmMy year of commits, by weekday and by month.
  3. 03TideThe waves between pages, with the controls exposed.
  4. 04TonesThe four sounds of this site, and one octave.
  5. 05PaletteThe colours of the theme you are looking at.
  6. 06The dogA shiba that crosses the screen.

01A seeded run

Modelly exists to make runs reproducible. The same idea fits in 100 lines of JavaScript, with a Schelling model where each agent moves when fewer than 45 % of its neighbours match it.

The model needs JavaScript.

02Commit rhythm

The skyline on the home page shows one bar a day. Here the same year is added up by weekday, then by month.

    This part needs JavaScript.

    03Tide

    The waves that cover the screen between two pages are three sine curves that slide at different speeds. These sliders change them.

    This part needs JavaScript.

    04Tones

    The site has sound, off by default. It uses four short tones made in the browser, with no audio file. The keys below play them, and the second row is one octave.

    05Palette

    The colours of the theme you are looking at. Switch theme at the top and they change. Press one to copy its value.

      06The dog

      Type shiba on any page and a dog runs across the screen. The button does the same for people without a keyboard.
